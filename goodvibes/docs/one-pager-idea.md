GoodVibes — see inside sealed containers, without touching them

ONE LINE. GoodVibes reads what is inside a sealed, opaque container from the microscopic vibrations of its wall, using a laser and an ordinary camera, and turns that into an autonomous inspection line with an AI inspector that explains every call.

PROBLEM. Conventional vision reads surfaces. In sterile injectables the incumbent 100% in-line method (laser headspace analysis) reads only the gas above the product and needs a few millimetres of headspace to exist. The product itself — low-headspace fills, lyophilised cake state, particulates, and ultimately viscosity/aggregation in biologics — is invisible to the installed base. A biologic vial is worth thousands; a rejected lot, millions; inspection is mandated by USP <1207>, not ROI-justified.

PHYSICS. A coherent laser on a surface produces speckle; a defocused camera turns sub-micron tilts of that surface into pixel shifts. Measured on a 10×10 laser grid under a 50–1000 Hz chirp, the wall's vibration spectra encode the mass, stiffness and damping of whatever is inside (Kichler, Bagon, Sheinin — ICCV 2025). Vibrometers that measure this exist (Polytec, Optomet); nobody sells the inference. These vibrations exist in any video (MIT's visual microphone, 2014); for a sealed container the signal is ~1000× smaller, so today we amplify it with a $5 laser — the roadmap is to remove it.

PROTOTYPE (built in 24 h). Data: Weizmann "laser-vibrations" — 551 recordings of a sealed cardboard box with a hidden object at ~130 positions on an 11×12 grid, 4 excitation sources, one empty-box reference per source. Model: our PyTorch Vibration Transformer (per-point spectral tokens → PointTransformer → ShapeTransformer over the laser grid → position, confidence, occupancy heatmap), fed differential measurements (minus the same-excitation empty reference). Held out by physics. Result on S3 (fixed line excitation, unseen position/excitation combinations): 1.34 cells mean localisation error, 43% within one cell, 21% exact — vs gradient boosting 1.42, kNN 1.70, ridge 1.73, random ≈ 3.8. S1 (never-heard excitation) reported honestly as the open hard case. Product: an autonomous line monitor — every container auto-inspected, PASS/FLAG with a reject queue, batch-record CSV and QA alert; click any container for the heatmap, ground-truth reveal, per-laser spectrum vs reference, and the Gemini report.

GEMINI, LOAD-BEARING. gemini-3.8-flash as a zero-training few-shot inspector scored on the same held-out set; gemini-embedding-2 + kNN as a second zero-training baseline; a function-calling inspection agent that calls our model as a tool and writes a structured QC report citing the laser points and frequency bands that drove the call; a multimodal self-audit of the predicted heatmap against the overhead photo. Built with Antigravity CLI (agy); hosted on Cloud Run; secrets in Secret Manager.

GOOGLE TOOLS. Antigravity CLI, Gemini API (gemini-3.8-flash, gemini-embedding-2), Google AI Studio, Cloud Run, Cloud Build, Secret Manager.

WHY NOW. The founding paper is four months old, its code is unreleased, and the lab's closed-package dataset appeared this autumn. GoodVibes is the first inference product on top of it. Next: a design partner in fill-finish; fill level and lyo-cake state are the same measurement with new labels.

HONEST SCOPE. Proved tonight: hidden contents located through a sealed opaque wall, non-contact. Same measurement, next labels: fill level, lyo cake, particulates. Frontier: viscosity/aggregation, ripeness/spoilage, equipment health. Industrial and pharmaceutical inspection only.
